# Spoonfish Software

Welcome. If you're seeing this page, someone at Spoonfish invited you in — as an employee, a contractor, a collaborator, or a friend of the company. This note is meant to orient you.

## Who we are

Spoonfish is a small, early-stage software company. We're building one thing at a time, carefully, and we'd rather ship something we're proud of than something we're finished with.

## How this org works

- **Small by design.** Membership is curated. If you're here, you have a reason to be.
- **Written down beats spoken.** Decisions that matter live in the repo that made them — as architecture records, PR discussions, or commit messages — not in chat.
- **Pull requests are the unit of work.** Review is how we teach, catch mistakes, and stay aligned. Expect feedback; give it generously.
- **Private until it isn't.** Most repos here are private. Don't share code, screenshots, or internal writing outside the org without asking.

## Finding your way around

Each repo has its own `README` explaining what it does and how to run it. If you don't have access to a repo you think you should, ask the person who invited you.

## Not an engineer?

You still belong here. Some work in this org isn't code — design, product thinking, research, writing, operations. You don't need to read the source to contribute. If GitHub's interface feels foreign, the person who invited you can walk you through the parts you need.

## Questions

Ask the person who invited you. They'll either answer or point you at someone who can.
